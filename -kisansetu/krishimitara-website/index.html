THE OPPORTUNITY

Farming takes a village.
Let's connect it.

Useful farming services can be spread across different places, people and apps. Krishimitara aims to bring the ecosystem closer together—so farmers can discover support, service providers can reach customers, and organisations can work together.

Our vision is to grow in phases, starting with practical daily utilities and expanding through trusted local partnerships.
