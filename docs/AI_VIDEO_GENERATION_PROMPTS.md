## 🚀 How to Produce These Videos in 3 Easy Steps

1. **Copy the Gemini Prompt** → Paste into [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) to receive the full word-for-word script, timing cues, and natural voiceover text in Gujarati or Hindi.
2. **Generate the Voiceover** → Paste the Gemini script into [Google Cloud Text-to-Speech](https://cloud.google.com/text-to-speech) (or ElevenLabs / Murf.ai) selecting native Gujarati / Hindi neural voices.
3. **Generate Video Clips** → Copy the **Google Veo / AI Video Prompt** into your AI video tool (Google VideoFX / Veo, Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine, or Sora) to produce photorealistic 4K clips, then assemble with your voiceover and background music.
