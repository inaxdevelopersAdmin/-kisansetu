## Stakeholder Value Propositions

### 1. Farmers (किसान)
- Instant access to farm financial health (Khata book) without waiting for internet.
- 95%+ accurate AI detection of leaf rust, blast, bollworm with precise chemical & biological cure recommendations.
- Elimination of exploitative middleman cuts through transparent transporter fares and mandi price awareness.

### 2. Agricultural Testing Laboratories
- Digital order management for soil, water, and seed germination testing.
- Automated generation of Soil Health Cards delivered straight to the farmer's mobile screen.

### 3. Rural Transporters & Drivers
- Discover nearby farm loads and scheduled mandi runs without idle tractor/truck returns.
- Transparent per-kilometer pricing and booking confirmations.

### 4. Government & Agricultural Universities
- Real-time early warning feeds for pest outbreaks and unseasonal weather.
- Rapid diffusion and direct application tracking for central & state schemes (PM-KISAN, KCC, Kusum).
